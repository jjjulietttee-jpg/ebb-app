Best Face Yoga App for iPhone: 21-Day Plan and an Honest Comparison

Updated October 2026 · By the Drena developer

Short answer. The best face yoga app is the one you will open daily. FaceYogi and Luvly are large, dedicated face yoga libraries. Drena is smaller: a 21-Day Face Yoga program that builds up from a three-day foundation to the full routine, alongside gua sha, drainage and a daily puffiness score.

Step by step

  1. Choose 21-Day Face YogaOpen Programs in Drena and pick 21-Day Face Yoga.
  2. Start with the foundationDays 1 to 3 are Face Yoga: First Three.
  3. Add exercises each weekThe program moves through five, seven, nine and eleven-exercise sets, then finishes with the full routine for the last three days.
  4. Aim for about ten minutesThe program is modelled on a published programme; Drena describes it as roughly ten minutes a day.
  5. Check your Morning ScoreLog how your face feels each morning to see whether the habit lines up with less puffiness for you.

Frequently asked questions

Does face yoga work?

Drena states the evidence modestly: in one 20-week study, women who kept at it had fuller-looking cheeks. Three weeks builds the habit and is not a result in itself.

How long is the program?

21 days: 3 foundation days, then 4, 4, 4, 3 and 3 days at increasing exercise counts.

Is Drena a pure face yoga app?

No. It also has gua sha, drainage routines for face, belly, legs and arms, a Morning Score and a trigger map.

How many routines are there?

26 drawn routines in total across face, belly, legs, hands and full body.

Do I get exercise videos?

The routines are drawn rather than filmed, with the stroke direction shown on the body.

What does it cost?

Prices come from the App Store and vary by country; see the listing or the in-app paywall. There is a free trial of Drena Pro.

Is it a medical product?

No. It is a wellness app and not a medical device.
