How to Do Lymphatic Drainage on Your Face at Home (With an iPhone App)

Updated October 2026 · By the Drena developer

Short answer. Face drainage is a very light, slow stroke that always moves toward the ears and then down the side of the neck to the collarbone. Drena guides this as a 3-minute morning routine on iPhone, with every stroke drawn as a path with an arrowhead so you can see the direction. It is a wellness routine for everyday puffiness, not a medical treatment.

Step by step

  1. Start with clean hands or a gua sha stone and a little oilFacial routines in Drena run by hand or with a gua sha stone. The route is the same; the grip, the pressure and the safety notes differ.
  2. Clear the neck firstOpen the Face morning routine in Drena. It begins by clearing the neck, so fluid from the face has somewhere to go.
  3. Stroke the jaw to the ear, both sidesFollow the drawn path from the chin along the jaw to the ear. Use feather-light pressure and slow strokes.
  4. Stroke the cheeks to the ears, both sidesMove from the cheeks toward the ears, again following the arrow on screen.
  5. Finish by clearing the neck againSweep from behind the ear down the side of the neck to the collarbone. Lymph moves toward nodes, never away from them, so the final direction is down.

Frequently asked questions

Does lymphatic drainage detox the body?

No. Lymph does not flush toxins; your liver and kidneys do that. Face drainage moves fluid that is already there, which is why the effect is short-lived and why Drena frames puffiness as fluid, not toxins.

How long does a face drainage routine take in Drena?

The Face morning routine is 3 minutes. The gua sha routines run 3 to 5 minutes.

Which direction should face drainage strokes go?

Toward the ear and then down the side of the neck to the collarbone. In Drena every stroke is drawn on the face as a path with an arrowhead because the direction is the part that matters.

Do I need a gua sha stone?

No. The facial routines run by hand or with a stone. The route is identical; pressure and grip differ.

How hard should I press?

Very lightly. The morning de-puff routine in Drena uses feather pressure on most strokes, and the under-eye stroke is described as barely touching.

How long does the effect last?

Hours, not weeks. Drainage moves fluid that is already there, so Drena treats puffiness as a daily thing and gives you a daily Morning Score instead of a number that climbs forever.

Is this medical advice?

No. Drena is a wellness app for everyday puffiness. It does not diagnose or treat anything, including lymphoedema. If swelling is sudden, one-sided or painful, see a doctor.
