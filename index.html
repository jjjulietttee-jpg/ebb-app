Frequently asked questions

What is the best app for morning face puffiness?

It depends on what you want. FaceYogi and Luvly are large face yoga libraries; Depuff AI analyses photos. Drena focuses on lymphatic drainage and gua sha routines, a daily Morning Score and finding your own triggers.

Does Drena scan my face with the camera?

No. There is no AI face scan or camera analysis.

Does lymphatic drainage detox the body?

No. Lymph does not flush toxins; the liver and kidneys do. Drena treats puffiness as fluid.

Is Drena free?

It is free to download and has a free trial of Drena Pro. Prices are shown in the app.

Is Drena available on Android?

No, Drena is an iPhone app.

Is it a medical device?

No. It does not diagnose or treat anything, including lymphoedema. See a doctor for sudden, one-sided or painful swelling.
